Workstation setup

Install Orchard

Set up the side-panel assistant on your computer. About five minutes, and you never reinstall.

No login to download Updates itself ~5 minutes
Before you start: your setup contact has already installed Chrome, Git, and Node on this computer, and sent you the enroll script (enroll.ps1 on Windows, enroll.sh on Mac). Save it to your Desktop.
This computer is
1

Run the one setup command

Open PowerShell (press the Windows key, type PowerShell, press Enter), then paste this in exactly and press Enter:

Open Terminal (press ⌘+Space, type Terminal, press Enter), then paste this in and press Enter:

powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File "$HOME\Desktop\enroll.ps1" -RepoUrl https://github.com/flyingturtlesolutions/orchard.git
bash ~/Desktop/enroll.sh https://github.com/flyingturtlesolutions/orchard.git
You'll see green text with your next two steps and a folder path. Keep that window open.
2

Add it to Chrome

  1. Open Chrome. In the address bar type chrome://extensions and press Enter.
  2. Turn on “Developer mode” (the toggle at the top-right).
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose the folder the green text showed you.
“Orchard” appears in your list of extensions.
3

Open it and turn on logging

  1. Click the puzzle-piece icon (top-right of Chrome) and pin Orchard.
  2. Click the Orchard icon to open its side panel.
  3. In the panel’s settings, turn on cloud logs and sign in when asked.
The Orchard panel opens on the right side of Chrome.

That’s it — you’re set up

Orchard keeps itself up to date. When a new version is ready, a small reload button lights up in the panel — click it to apply. Otherwise it updates quietly in the background, and you never reinstall.

Something look wrong? An error, no green text, or the extension won’t load — stop, take a photo of the screen, and message your setup contact. Don’t keep clicking.
