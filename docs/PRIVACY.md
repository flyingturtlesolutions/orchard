# Orchard — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-08-18_

Orchard is a Chrome browser extension provided by **Deako, Inc.** ("Deako", "we"). This policy describes what
data the Orchard extension handles and where it goes. It supplements Deako's general privacy policy at
<https://www.deako.com/legal/>, which governs Deako as the data controller; for matters specific to the
extension, this policy applies.

## What Orchard does with page content

Orchard is an assistant that helps you operate the websites you work in. When you engage it on a page, it reads
that page's content and structure so it can understand the page and carry out the request you make. It reads and
transmits page content **only to fulfil a request you initiate** — it does not passively monitor or collect your
browsing.

## Where your data goes

- **Anthropic.** To produce a response, Orchard sends your request and the relevant page content to the Anthropic
  API (Anthropic, PBC), the AI service that powers the assistant. Anthropic processes it to return a response;
  see Anthropic's privacy terms at <https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy>.
- **Deako cloud (optional).** If you enable the optional cloud features (sync and diagnostic logging), the same
  data may be sent to and stored in Deako's cloud service on Amazon Web Services. Diagnostic logs are **scrubbed
  of identifying values** (such as emails, phone numbers, and record identifiers) on your device before they are
  sent.
- If you do not enable the optional cloud features, no account is required, and no data leaves your machine
  except the calls to Anthropic needed to answer you.

## Stored on your device

Your saved site knowledge, settings, and history are kept locally in your browser's storage. They are not sent
anywhere unless you enable the optional cloud features above.

## What we do not do

We do not sell your data, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not use it for creditworthiness or lending
decisions.

## Data retention

Deako retains personal data for no longer than is reasonably necessary for its processing purpose, per Deako's
general privacy policy at <https://www.deako.com/legal/>.
